How it works

From folder to feed, in three steps.

Your footage stays where it lives. Automation reads only what you point it to, shapes it, and sends the finished cuts where they belong.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Link a Google account with read-only access. Automation can open your videos, and can't change or delete anything in your Drive.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Trim the moment, crop to the subject and reframe one master video into every aspect ratio your channels expect.

  3. 03

    Publish

    Send each finished cut to the social platforms you choose, on your schedule, with a clear record of what went where.

Google Drive

Drive access, in plain words.

Connecting a Google account is the only way Automation reaches your files. Here is exactly what that allows, and what it never will.

With your permission

What Automation can do

  • See your name, email address and profile photo, to show which account is connected.
  • Read files in your Google Drive, so the videos you choose can be edited and published.
  • Record when an account is connected, checked or disconnected, for security.

Never

What it will never do

  • Edit, move or delete anything in your Google Drive.
  • Sell your data, or use it for advertising.
  • Use your files to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Let people read your files, except to provide the service, for security, or where the law requires it.

Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in our privacy policy.

Security

Quiet by design.

The least access needed, protected the way it should be, and easy to take back.

256-bit

Google access tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and never shown in the portal.

Read-only

Automation asks Google only for read-only Drive access, the least it needs to do its job.

One click

Disconnect an account in the portal, or revoke access from your Google Account, at any time.
