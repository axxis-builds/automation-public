The short version

  • Automation reads files in a connected Google Drive only to edit and publish the videos you choose. It never changes or deletes anything there.
  • We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use Google data to train AI models.
  • Google access tokens are encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • You can disconnect a Google account at any time, and ask us to access or delete your data.

Who we are

Automation is a social media automation platform operated by Axxis Builds ("we", "us", "our"). It lets authorised team members take videos from Google Drive, edit and reframe them, and publish them to social media channels. Axxis Builds is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • the Automation admin portal and the services behind it;
  • the Google accounts connected to Automation, and the Google Drive data it accesses; and
  • this website.

Social media platforms you publish to, and Google itself, have their own privacy policies, which apply to how they handle your information.

Information we collect

We collect only what Automation needs to work and to stay secure:

CategoryWhat it includes
Portal accounts Email address, first and last name, roles and permissions, and your password, which we store only as a one-way hash and can never read.
Sign-in sessions When you sign in and out, your IP address and browser details (user agent), used to keep sessions secure and let you sign out other devices.
Connected Google accounts The Google account's ID, email address, name and profile photo, the permissions it granted, encrypted access and refresh tokens, its connection status, and which portal user connected it and when.

Activity records When Google accounts are connected, reconnected, renamed, checked or disconnected, who did it, and their IP address and browser details.
Published content The edited videos, captions and publishing details you send to social media platforms through Automation.

Google user data

When you connect a Google account, Google asks you to allow the following, and Automation uses each only as described:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo (openid, email, profile): to identify the connected account and show it in the portal.
  • Read-only access to your Google Drive (drive.readonly): to open the video files you choose so they can be edited and published. Automation cannot create, change, move or delete files in your Drive.

When you ask Automation to edit or publish a video, it reads that file and may make a temporary working copy to process it. Working copies are deleted once they are no longer needed for that task. We don't make copies of your Drive for any other purpose.

Automation's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, we:

  • use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you use in Automation;
  • transfer it to others only as needed to provide those features (for example, publishing a video you chose), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you;
  • never use it for advertising, and never sell it;
  • never use it to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models; and
  • don't allow people to read it unless you have given permission for specific content, it is needed for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), it is required by law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

How we use information

  • To provide Automation: signing you in, connecting Google accounts, editing videos and publishing them where you choose.
  • To keep Automation secure: checking permissions on every request, detecting misuse, and keeping an activity record of account connections.
  • To operate and support the service, and to answer your questions.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

How we share information

  • Social media platforms you choose. When you publish, the video and details you selected are sent to those platforms.
  • Service providers. Companies that host or run our infrastructure on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing those services.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, Axxis Builds or others.
  • Business transfers. If Axxis Builds is involved in a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

Storage and security

  • Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and are never sent to the portal or shown to anyone.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Every request is checked against the user's permissions; only authorised administrators can connect or disconnect Google accounts.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a breach as required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Google tokens are deleted as soon as an account is disconnected.
  • Working copies of videos are deleted once the task they were made for is finished.
  • Portal accounts are kept while the account is in use, and deleted on request or when no longer needed.
  • Activity and session records are kept for security and audit purposes, and deleted when no longer needed for them.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect Google accounts in the portal under Storage → Google Drive. This revokes Automation's access at Google and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Revoke access from Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing privacy@axxis.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including data portability and the right to complain to a data protection authority.

We respond to requests within a reasonable time, and within any period the law requires.

Cookies

The Automation portal uses only essential cookies: one keeps you signed in, and a short-lived one secures the Google connection process. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

This website sets no cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services may receive your IP address when you visit, under their own privacy policies.

Children

Automation is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Automation changes. We will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will notify users before they take effect.
